<CourseCallout title="Why this course exists">
  Modern AI products are no longer single prompts attached to a model endpoint. They are systems with retrieval, memory, tools, orchestration, evaluation, and operational constraints. This course teaches students how to reason about that full system stack.
</CourseCallout>

  title="By the end of the course, students will be able to"

    'Evaluate system quality, failure modes, and robustness under real constraints.',
