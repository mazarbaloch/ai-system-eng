<CourseCallout title="Curriculum design">
  The sequence is intentionally cumulative. Early modules establish the vocabulary for AI systems, middle modules introduce retrieval and tool use, and later modules focus on reliability, optimization, and operations. By the capstone, students have seen the building blocks in isolation and as an integrated system.
</CourseCallout>
